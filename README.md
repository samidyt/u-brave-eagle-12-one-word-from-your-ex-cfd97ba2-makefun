Entry for the make.fun bounty [One Word From Your Ex](https://make.fun/bounty/u-brave-eagle-12-one-word-from-your-ex-cfd97ba2): send exactly one word, the word your ex would use to describe you, and nothing else.

## The word

**Project**

An ex doesn't call you selfish and move on. She calls you a project, something she spent months trying to fix before quietly giving up and putting you back on the shelf. It stings because it's true: you weren't a partner, you were a renovation, and she didn't leave you, she abandoned the worksite.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the word to `entry.txt` (to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set, otherwise `out/`). No network, no dependencies, Node stdlib only.

Run it: `node main.ts`
